Write on instinct.

An idea shows up. By the time you've unlocked, found your notes app and opened a new page, it's gone. Instink is a blank sheet on your Home Screen: tap it and you're already writing, by hand.

Tap.The sheet is ready in under half a second. No account, no choices to make.
Write.In your own handwriting, with your finger or Apple Pencil. Or type, snap a photo, or speak.
Done.You're back where you were. Later, send the note to Notion, Keep, Notes or anywhere.

Your notes stay yours

No account. No ads. No tracking. Your notes are stored on your phone and never pass through a server of ours. Handwriting and speech are recognized on the device.
